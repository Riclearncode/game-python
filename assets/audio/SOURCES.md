# Audio Sources

## Bundled In The Repo

- Kenney RPG Audio, CC0: `assets/audio/kenney_rpg`
  - Used for UI clicks, coins, build/repair, hit, poison, hurt, wave and dash sounds.
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects
- OpenGameArt Monster Sound Effects Pack, CC0: `assets/audio/opengameart`
  - Used for zombie groan, attack, death and Titan roar.
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-effects-pack
- OpenGameArt 8bit SFX, CC0: `assets/audio/opengameart`
  - Included as an extra UI/retro confirm source.
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/8bit-sfx
- OpenGameArt Chunky Explosion, CC0: `assets/audio/opengameart`
  - Used as the Boomer explosion SFX.
  - Source: https://opengameart.org/content/chunky-explosion
- Project synth audio, generated by `tools/generate_synth_audio.py`: `assets/audio/synth`
  - Used for current weapon shots, reload, level-up, weapon upgrade and looping BGM.

## Supported External Sources

- Mixkit
  - Good for drop-in gun, whoosh, ambience and music replacements.
  - License page: https://mixkit.co/license/
- Sonniss GDC Bundle
  - Good for high-quality monster, cinematic, impact and environment replacements.
  - License page: https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/
  - Not bundled directly because the official bundles are very large.

If you download Mixkit or Sonniss replacements later, keep their original license/readme files next to the assets before committing them.
